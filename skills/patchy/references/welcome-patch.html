Welcome — this page is yours.

Your assistant just published it in your company's cloud, at its own link — ready to share with your colleagues.

A patchy page is a simple web page your assistant makes for you: a plan, a report, a proposal, anything worth showing someone. Instead of attaching a file or pasting a wall of text, you send a link that looks good on any screen.

  • Shareable

    Send the link to a colleague

    New pages are shared with your company by default: colleagues sign in to read them. Ask your assistant to make a page public when anyone with the link should be able to open it, or to share it with the company again.

  • Updatable

    The link stays, the page evolves

    Ask your assistant to change a page and it updates in place. Everyone you already sent the link to simply sees the newest version.

  • Long-lived

    Keep it live or put it away

    Pages do not expire from inactivity. Retire a page to take it offline while keeping its versions, tables and files indefinitely. You or a company admin can restore it at the same link whenever it is needed again.

  • Deletable

    Delete with time to recover

    Ask your assistant to delete a page and it goes offline. You or a company admin can restore it within 30 days. After that, its page, versions, tables, files and name are reclaimed for good.

Try it: next time you have a plan or a write-up, tell your assistant publish this with patchy — and you'll get a link like this one back.
