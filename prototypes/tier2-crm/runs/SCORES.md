## Checks common to all four (same result on each)

- **CSV import** (owner, direct call, `data/contacts.csv` on an empty CRM): 140 added, 10 companies created, 7 rejected and 5 skipped. Every rejected line and reason and every skipped line matches `expected-import.json` exactly. A re-import gives 0 added, 7 rejected and 145 skipped. Re-importing through each UI shows the counts and a per-line table. Everything imported is owned by `usr_dev`.
- **Ownership, by direct call as the colleague on the owner's records**: update, delete and transfer-to-self on a company, a contact and a public deal are all refused (`not_owner`/`owner_only`). On a private deal they return `not_found`. The owner editing the colleague's deal is refused too.
- **Handover**: a contact, a company and a deal handed to the colleague can then be edited by the colleague but not by the previous owner. Handing a deal to a non-member is refused (`unknown_member`/`unknown_teammate`). A private deal handed over becomes the colleague's and disappears for the old owner.
- **Private deals**: the colleague calls every read handler (get/detail, list, pipeline, company page, per-company deals, search, attachments list/stat/status, members) and the private deal's id never appears. The colleague's open board never shows it, while the owner's board shows it within ~100 ms.
- **Attachments**: owner stages → attach action → the colleague lists the file and redeems their own handle (bytes match). Redeeming the owner's handle as the colleague is refused (403 `access_denied`), including the private deal's handle. The colleague's attach and remove on the owner's deal are refused. Reusing an Upload, or using the owner's Upload as the colleague, gives `not_found`. Attaching through the UI works on all four, and the colleague, with the deal already open, sees the file live and downloads identical bytes.
- **Contracts**: each run declared `bhqb6nld1vtz/documents`, and the Acme company page shows the thumbnail (blob URL) and a working PDF download (787 bytes, `%PDF-`). After `replace acme-robotics` the thumbnail changes on the open colleague page within ~100 ms, the old handle gives 404 `not_found` and the new one gives 200. After `unshare` the contract panel shows an error, the direct call returns `access_denied` with `refusedIn: handler`, the old handle gives 403, the page is not replaced and deal reads still work. After `reshare` the panel is back within ~100 ms. Then `reset`.
- **Live pipeline**: a new deal and a move made by direct call reach the colleague's open board within ~100 ms (poll granularity). A move made through the owner's card `<select>` also reaches it.
- **Hidden tab**: the colleague's document was hidden for 33 s, the owner moved the deal while it was hidden, and the colleague's board caught up 54–57 ms after it became visible again.
- **Platform gap, the same in all four**: a handle minted while a deal was public still redeems (200) after the owner makes it private (checked on a-opus: `files.forDeal` gives `not_found`, but the old handle gives 200). Only the two astra runs found and documented this. Any run could have closed it by re-putting a deal's files when it goes private.

## a-opus (92)

Passed: everything common above. Attach is stage → `put` → `ctx.run.files.record`, and unrecorded files are listed with Retry/Remove. `deals.remove` deletes the files first and reports how far it got.

Failed or deducted:

- Finance: `finance.report` returns 8 of 10 companies. Ironwood Builders and Juniper Schools (0/0/0 in the truth) are missing because the SQL groups invoices only. The 8 rows present match to the cent. (−3)
- The board and company page round money to whole dollars (`$12,346` for 1,234,567 cents). Lists are capped at 1,000 rows with no paging. (−1)
- Forced follow-up failure: I raced `deals.destroy` against a 9 MB `files.attach`. The action returned `{stored: true, recorded: false}` and the page's warning is honest about that. It also promises the file is "listed below as 'not recorded'", but with the deal gone `files.forDeal` gives `not_found`, so those bytes are orphaned and unreachable. (−1, line 3)
- Usability: the unshare message is the raw "Contracts are unavailable: Runtime request refused: access_denied." and the board shows no cents. The code is small, commented and easy to extend. (−3, line 4)

Bugs: the orphaned bytes above. REPORT's finance claim ("per-company totals") leaves out companies with no invoices. The report never mentions the stale-handle gap.

## a-astra (95)

Passed: everything common above. The finance action matches all 10 companies exactly, including the zero rows, with exact numeric-to-text cents. The attachment store is the record (`dealId/token/name`), so there is no follow-up mutation that could fail after the put. A put error is reconciled with `stat`, and a lost reply with "Check last upload status". The status label shows "Reconnecting…"/"Connecting…" rather than "Connected" while hidden. Its unshare copy is the clearest of the four: "Access is unavailable. The source may no longer be shared with you." Paging and prefix search are on the server.

Failed or deducted:

- `deals.remove` deletes the row but not its files, so the bytes and any handles already minted outlive the deal. The confirmation dialog warns about this. (−1, line 2)
- Maintainability: `src/App.tsx` is one 3,324-character JSX line, 7 lines exceed 400 characters, there are 6 comment lines in 1,213, and 1,342 lines of CSS. The next agent has to reformat it before they can read it. (−4, line 4)

Bugs: orphaned files on deal delete (documented in its REPORT). One `save` mutation per record type handles create, edit and handover (through an optional `ownerId`). That is compact, but it overloads the mutation.

## b-opus (92)

Passed: everything common above. `shared/access.ts` (`visibleDeal`, `assertOwner`, `canSee`) is the clearest server pattern of the four. The pipeline reads through `byPrivate`/`byOwnerPrivate` indexes, so other people's private rows never leave Postgres. Contacts are paged on the server. Money is exact. The unshare copy is plain: "You no longer have access to this."

Failed or deducted:

- Finance: the same 8-of-10 omission as a-opus. (−3)
- Race: `attachments.attach` racing `deals.remove` at delay 0 returned `{stored: true, recorded: true}`, while `remove` reported `{filesRemoved: 0, dealRemoved: true}`. The action's `record()` never re-checks that the deal exists, so the user is told "Attached" on a deleted deal, and the file and record are left orphaned. (−2, line 3; −1, line 2 for the orphan)
- Variant B's packages were never imported. It uses native `<dialog>`, `<select>` and plain tables, so this run says nothing about Zag or TanStack.

Bugs: the race above. It imports types from `patchy/server` although the skill points to the generated module. REPORT says the partial-failure path was never exercised, which is honest.

## b-astra (94)

Passed: everything common above. Finance matches all 10 exactly, including the zero rows (bigint to text cents, paged). The store is the attachment record, as in a-astra, and a new upload refuses an existing key. The pipeline is one snapshot query over `byPublicStage`/`byOwnerStage`. The CSV parser handles BOM, CRLF and multiline fields and has limits.

Failed or deducted:

- `deals.remove` (mutation) never deletes files. The UI makes you empty the list first, but a direct call leaves orphaned bytes. (−1, line 2)
- Usability: attaching takes "Stage selected file" then "Attach staged file", and the panel shows raw file keys and a "Reconcile a file key" input. Those are platform mechanics a sales rep should not see. The unshare copy is the raw "Contracts unavailable: Runtime request refused: access_denied." CSS is minified into 8 lines, 14 lines exceed 400 characters, and at 161 KB (48.7 KB gzip) it is the largest page, because of Zag, which it uses only for one dialog. (−5, line 4)

Bugs: the direct-delete orphan. Pipeline status maps `suspended` to "Connecting…", which is not wrong but not explicit either.

## Stack findings (not charged to any run)

- **Client disconnect leaks the invocation.** Aborting the HTTP request of an in-flight `server.call` leaves its `runtime_calls` row `pending` and its company-database connection `idle in transaction` past both the 10 s handler deadline and the 30 s service deadline. I reproduced it once on purpose (`abort.mjs`: abort after 40 ms on `imports.importCsv`, still leaked 85 s later). Four leaks (18:53:46 `integrations.companyContracts`, 18:58:52 `imports.importCsv`, 18:59:00 `imports.contacts`, 18:59:58 `deals.pipeline`, each from a browser I closed mid-call) filled the company's four slots, and from 19:01 every call timed out at 30 s. `pg_terminate_backend` did not free the slots; only `pnpm dev stop` and a restart did.
- A private file handle is not revoked when its record changes visibility (above). This needs a platform answer, or a skill line telling agents to re-put files when access narrows.
- Neither B run showed variant B paying for itself. b-opus imported no B package, and b-astra imported only Zag dialog, at +64 KB HTML (+24 KB gzip) over the A runs.

## State left behind

The fixtures are reshared and reset: contracts v15 is shared and Acme's PDF is the original 787 bytes. The instance is running and healthy after the restart. The published CRMs now hold the imported contacts plus the verifier's test deals, attachments and handovers. The a-astra and b-astra builders left their `patchy dev` workerd processes running (pids 727489 and 824847, plus children); I did not touch them.

## Pick

**a-astra** is the example agents should learn from. It and b-astra are the only runs that matched every truth figure (the import, all 10 finance rows to the cent, the private deal in every handler, replace/unshare/reshare), and a-astra wins between them because it stays on variant A at 97 KB, and keeps staging mechanics out of the UI; like b-astra, its attachment store is the record, so no follow-up step can fail. Before it becomes the reference, run a formatter over it, add comments to b-opus's `shared/access.ts` standard, and make `deals.remove` delete the deal's files.
