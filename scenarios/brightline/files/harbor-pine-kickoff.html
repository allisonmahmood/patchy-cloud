Brightline Studio · Kickoff brief

Harbor & Pine Coffee: a brand that tastes like the coast.

Harbor & Pine has grown from one roastery café to 14 locations and a fast-growing online subscription. The brand hasn't kept up. This brief aligns the studio and the client on what we're making, why, and by when.

What success looks like

01

Stand out on a crowded shelf

Grocery buyers in two regional chains asked for packaging that reads from two metres away. We'll test shelf presence against the top five competitors before we lock the system.

02

One brand from café to inbox

Today the cafés, the bags and the subscription emails look like three companies. A single identity, voice and photography direction ties them together.

03

Grow subscriptions 30% by summer

The client's headline metric. The new unboxing and onboarding emails are designed around it, and we'll track it with their team from launch.

Scope

In scope

  • Brand strategy, positioning and naming architecture for the product range
  • Visual identity: wordmark, symbol, colour, type, illustration style
  • Packaging system for 18 SKUs with print-ready artwork
  • Café signage kit and menu templates
  • Brand guidelines and a handover workshop for their in-house team

Out of scope (for now)

  • Website rebuild: proposed as a follow-on in January
  • Photography production: we art-direct, they commission
  • Interior fit-out of the cafés
  • Media buying for the launch campaign
